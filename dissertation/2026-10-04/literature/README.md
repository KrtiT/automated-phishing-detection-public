# Citation source-check record

The repository-linked manuscript contains 59 references: the preceding 58
scholarly/dataset entries plus the versioned research record, Tallam (2026).
The integration pass checks 86 body citation/reference links, with no uncited
bibliography entry. The earlier October 4 expansion added seven primary
references and checked 85 links. The
[claim ledger](source-claims.json) records the claim supported by each new
reference and the part of the source actually inspected.

Five new sources were checked in relevant full-text sections. Axelsson (2000)
and Dean and Barroso (2013) were checked through bibliographic metadata and
abstracts, not their complete articles. This is not a claim that all 58 existing
references underwent a full-text audit.

[Initial retrieval metadata](source-retrieval.json) and
[additional retrieval metadata](source-retrieval-additional.json) preserve
source URLs, retrieval failures and available hashes. Named `sources/` files
are local evidence locators, not bundled papers. Downloaded publisher/author
article copies and extracted full text are deliberately not redistributed here.

Citation verification supports attribution and claim checking. It is not a
Turnitin report, an AI-detector guarantee or institutional academic-integrity
clearance.
