AGGREGATE DATA DICTIONARY — October 2, 2026

GENERAL

Three separate namespaces: aggregate-data/original/, detection-D/, service-S/.
Never concatenate them as one test population or one operational schedule.
Original and D data concern labeled URL records; S uses synthetic service inputs
and supplies no detection-accuracy estimate. All CSVs have UTF-8 headers.
JSON preserves nested structure, full precision, nulls and reasons.
Rates are fractions unless the field name explicitly says otherwise; multiply
by 100 for percentages. Rate differences times 100 are percentage points.
Latencies/durations are milliseconds; throughput is requests per second.
Blank fields mean absent/inapplicable/undefined, NEVER a measured zero.
Boolean CSVs use True/False. Hash fields use SHA-256.

ORIGINAL STUDY

See ORIGINAL_DATA_DICTIONARY.txt for every original field, population, model and
denominator. It is preserved from the October 1 package. The unchanged program
has 125 operational rows, 25 groups and 22 primary checks; 1,243,505 requests and
1,901 errors. Internal evaluation contains 34,593 rows and external strata 8,701.
Those source strata are not a deployment prevalence distribution. Complete
secondary outputs retain all originally reported metrics and limitations.

DETECTION D

Admitted benchmark: 8,622 records; 4,651 publisher positives, 3,971 publisher
negatives; 6,273 registrable domains. Largest domain: 351 rows. Excluding observed
domains does not prove temporal or source-mechanism independence. Opposite-scheme
companions are prescribed metamorphic pairs, not extra independent label samples.

detection-metrics.csv (2 rows): model baseline/candidate, class totals,
tp/fp/tn/fn, frozen threshold, recall, fpr, precision, roc_auc, average_precision,
brier. Baseline is the unchanged 25-feature structural model; candidate is the
transport-neutral 24-feature model. Confusion counts use their separate frozen
validation-selected thresholds; no benchmark retuning.

calibration-bins.csv (20 rows): model, bin, count, mean_probability,
positive_fraction. Ten fixed bins per model. Empty bins have undefined means
and observed fractions; they are not reliability points at zero.

scheme-invariance.csv (2 rows): model; exact_feature_matches when supplied;
exact_score_matches; decision_flips; max_absolute_score_difference; denominator.
All 8,622 candidate features/scores match, with zero decision flips. Baseline
has 481 flips. A flip is sensitivity, not automatically a classification error.

domain-size-distribution.csv (26 rows): rows_per_domain and domain_count.
Sum domain_count = 6,273; sum rows_per_domain * domain_count = 8,622.

verification.json: source/export hashes; full model metrics and confusion
counts; paired_uncertainty; scheme checks and D decision. Domain bootstrap:
10,000 resamples, PCG64 seed 20261001, row-weighted estimates after sampling
registrable domains. Two-sided 97.5% percentile intervals. Recall difference
is candidate minus baseline: -0.0395613846484627. D requires a >=0.05 gain,
positive lower interval bound and observed candidate FPR <=0.01. Exact scheme
invariance is separately reported conformance, not another efficacy test.

SERVICE S — COMPLETE V2 ONLY

Two workloads (no_model, structural_detector), two concurrencies (1, 64), ten
pairs and two clients (shared, worker) = 80 arms. Every arm has 1,000 warmups and
10,000 measured attempts. Fresh service per arm; alternating order within pairs.
The primary estimand uses structural_detector, concurrency 64, all ten pairs.
Synthetic strings are never fetched. No transformer is used in these arms.

arm-metrics.csv (80 rows, schedule order): workload/concurrency/pair/client
identify the arm; manifest_sha256 identifies ordered synthetic requests.
request_count includes errors. request_errors/error_rate describe terminal
client outcomes. p50_ms/p95_ms/p99_ms retain the original success-only summary.
all_latency_*, success_latency_* and failure_latency_* explicitly give counts
and p50/p95/p99 by outcome. Failure quantiles are blank for zero errors.
measured_elapsed_ms excludes warmup and drain; measured_drain_ms is separate.
client_attempts_per_second = all attempts / measured duration.
successful_responses_per_second = client successes / measured duration.
admitted_requests/completed_requests/failed_requests are server counter deltas;
successful_transformer_scores and transformer_forward_attempts are physical
inference counters (all zero here). error_types_* gives the six terminal client
categories. The only measured error is a shared structural c64 pair-2 timeout,
2019.144208 ms; it was not admitted. Server failed_requests is zero throughout.

group-metrics.csv (8 rows): one workload/concurrency/client combination per row,
ten arms, 100,000 measured attempts. Latency quantiles pool individual observations;
they are not averages of run percentiles. Throughput divides summed attempts or
successes by summed measured durations, not by an average rate. Physical counters
sum across arms. all_count/success_count/failure_count specify denominators.

pair-metrics.csv (40 rows): workload/concurrency/pair; shared_p95_ms and
worker_p95_ms; ratio = worker/shared success-only p95. requested = 10,000 pairs.
both_successful is the paired comparability denominator. exact_except_request_id
requires equality of response fields INCLUDING admission sequence, excluding
only request IDs. prediction_agreement additionally excludes sequence and is a
separate descriptive result; it does not substitute for the S agreement rule.
noncomparable_errors counts requested pairs missing a successful response.
Across all 40 pairs, 399,999/399,999 comparable predictions agree; 400,000 paired
comparisons were requested. Do not claim observed agreement on the missing pair.

primary-pairs.csv (10 rows): structural c64 subset of pair-metrics. There are
100,000 requested pairs, 99,999 comparable pairs and 99,999 prediction agreements;
strict equality is 799/100,000. Another 99,200 differ only by admission sequence.
One shared timeout is noncomparable. Primary worker errors: 0/100,000.

requirements.csv (5 rows): frozen requirement key and passed Boolean. Median
ratio <=0.8; 97.5% upper ratio bound <1; pooled worker success p95 <=200 ms;
worker error rate <0.001; exact responses apart from IDs for all requested pairs.
Four pass. S is the conjunction, so it is not supported.

verification.json: independent saved-evidence arithmetic, all source/aggregate
hashes, complete accounting, ten primary ratios, median, paired bootstrap,
agreement counts, sampled condition summary and exclusion of interrupted v1.
Median ratio: 0.20324898732971414; interval [0.18790304827391455,
0.21350823820542653]. Bootstrap: ten run pairs, 10,000 resamples, PCG64 seed
20261002. It does not resample 100,000 requests as independent runs.
D/S 97.5% intervals allocate Bonferroni coverage across two comparisons.

RECOVERY AND LIMITATIONS

The original S attempt stopped after 45 complete arms and part of the 46th
on observed AC loss. Its 450,000 completed measured requests are disclosed
separately, NEVER pooled into v2. The complete new schedule follows a dated
operator-approved amendment and sampled stable AC. Zero recorded v2 guard
violations does not prove absence of all unobserved disturbances. Service results
are conditional on this host and synthetic workload, not production capacity.
Hash-only inventories are provenance, not included raw data or access grants.
