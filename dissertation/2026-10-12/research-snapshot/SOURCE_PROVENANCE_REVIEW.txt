Data rights and target

The publisher declares the URL/features/labels/docs tier CC BY 4.0. Gated malicious
HTML/screenshots are outside this revision. Version 4 is documentation-only with
unchanged Version 3 data. Retained inputs must match the already recorded source
digest; documentation does not silently authorize a newer data release.

Gold is the publisher's source/confidence designation. Positives derive from the
NCSC anti-fraud feed, not this study's contemporaneous forensic examination or a
uniformly verified credential-harvesting task. Certified negatives come from the
trusted-organization/website registries. Preserve those labels and names without
upgrading them to independently adjudicated truth. Gold-source agreement and
source handling are evidence about provenance, not proof of zero label error.

The published audit reviewed 200 stratified records: 149 were resolvable and 51
unsure. The resolved positive errors were 12/99 (eleven bronze and one silver),
and benign errors were 2/50 (both silver/Tranco). These are audit-stratum counts;
the absence of a reported gold error in that sample does not certify every gold
record. Neither these results nor predictor behavior may be used to relabel
individual records in this revision.

Time, allocation and dependence

The NCSC source has no published event after February 18, 2025. Publisher split
assignment is domain-grouped and temporal for dated records, deterministic by
hash for undated records. Thus train/validation candidates are retrospective
held-out domains for our models, not future-time confirmation. collected_at is
a source event date, not necessarily the acquisition timestamp. No verified
campaign-ID field establishes independence across distinct registered domains.

The updated local exposure roster covers 58 files, 21 distinct contents and
207,528 registered historical domains, with zero unresolved identities in that
discovered roster. It includes the original group-test and interrupted attempts.
It does not establish nonexposure in unrecorded activity elsewhere. After its
exclusions, upper bounds are 1,617 positive URLs in 1,392 domains and 4,952
certified-negative URLs in 1,262 domains. There are no cross-label candidate
domains. All positive candidates are dated (61 dates); 3,523 negative rows lack
dates. Calendar dates are not verified campaign clusters.

Representation contract

Publisher documentation treats HTTPS as a collection artifact and uses
scheme-independent features. Its schema prose describes a scheme-stripped
url_norm, while the retained accepted inputs inspected by preparation contain
absolute HTTP/HTTPS strings. The retained data and tested parser, not an inferred
schema correction, determine the model input contract. Do not silently add,
strip or repair schemes. A controlled scheme intervention must explicitly
change only the prefix of an otherwise unchanged accepted string, preserve the
original, and distinguish a frozen-model contrast from D's refit/calibration.

Scientific decision

The retained cohort can support a separately labeled retrospective,
domain-balanced comparison after fixed selection and complete admission. It
cannot support an unconditional claim of independent campaigns, true field
prevalence, future collection, or error-free labels. Paired model-based intervals
must state independence and label assumptions, and fixed sensitivity analyses
must accompany rather than erase those assumptions. No prediction is authorized
by this metadata review; the whole-study execution barrier is still required.
