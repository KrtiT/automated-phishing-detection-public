AGGREGATE DATA DICTIONARY — 12 OCTOBER REVISION

All CSV headers are machine-readable. Rates and interval endpoints are fractions
unless a column explicitly says milliseconds (ms). Multiply fractions by 100 for
percentages or percentage-point differences. Blank CSV cells / JSON null mean
not defined or not applicable, never zero. No raw URLs, domains or row identifiers
from the new experiments are included.

F-formatting: stratum and spelling identify fixed-model cells. rows is the
denominator; alerts and decision_flips are integer counts. TP is meaningful only
for positive reference strata, FP/FPR for certified negatives; Tranco supplies
alerts, not verified benign outcomes. All characters after the scheme colon are
unchanged; fitting, scaler and threshold are fixed. Secondary positives and
Tranco retain their distinct label roles.

R-routing: one URL per domain; P=1392, N=1262, total=2654. Counts are paired across
systems. logical_calls are routing selections, not timed executions. Repairs and
harms compare against structural decisions within each reference class. FPR upper
bounds and paired intervals are conditional on independent labeled units, not
campaign-independence certificates. Ranking/AP reflect this retained frame.
R-paired-contrasts: positive paired cells, individual two-sided 95% exact-envelope
endpoints; primary_statements and approximate/date sensitivities remain in JSON.
The new recall-only statements do not change original H1–H3 decisions.

C-all-arms: 6 paths x 5 repetitions; 1000 measured requests and 100 excluded
warmups per arm. Counters are measured-phase differences. Summed scoring seconds
exclude HTTP, request generation and whole-arm setup. Quantiles use linear
interpolation. C-cost-summary reports medians of run quantiles and paired
summed-scoring-time ratios to structural, with observed min/max, not confidence
intervals. Five cyclic rotations are not fully position-balanced. The workload
has a designed 1% positive mixture, not estimated field prevalence. No C/S pooling.

P-all-seeds: seeds 42–46, original training partition with audited shuffled labels;
validation P=12486, N=20209. Iterations and label changes describe the fit audit.
AUC/AP use unchanged validation labels, which also select each operating point.
TP/FP therefore describe validation-selected thresholds, not held-out efficacy.
Small coefficients or probabilities near .5 do not imply chance ranking. These
five diagnostic fits do not establish a formal .05 permutation test or certify
historical label consumption. JSON retains score quantiles and scoring-audit scope.

corrected-primary-gates: preserves original status, metric, operator and threshold,
with corrected estimates, uncertainty, method and decision. Eight passes remain;
one original H3 recall-margin pass changes. Original joint hypotheses remain
unsupported. An observed agreement result is not a zero-uncertainty guarantee.

freeze-identities: exact hashes of permitted input/output identities and frozen
research commit/profile. These are identities, not access capabilities or public
record-level data. The retained local verification reconstructed counts, P linkage,
R membership/repairs/ranking and C quantiles/counters from saved observations.
Not every secondary F feature delta or R date/FPR summary was independently
recomputed by that verifier. Aggregate consistency is weaker than independent
reproduction of private observations. Historical data/code remain in the separate
research-record-2026-10-04 release, subject to its own license and privacy record.
