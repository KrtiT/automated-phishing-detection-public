Interpretation

Report individual outcomes, including unexpected ones; do not demand exactly .5
ROC AUC, zero recall or a particular FPR. Any ranking signal calls for a traced
explanation before claims about leakage. Five permutations are not a .05-level
permutation test (even a valid corrected Monte Carlo test would have minimum
p=1/6). No new hypothesis threshold or pass criterion is created here.

Acceptance is a reproducible, contemporaneously observed execution plus consistent
disk evidence. Hashes alone are not proof of chronology, producer exit or lawful
access. The bounded worker and verifier implement evidence handling; neither is
the missing final execution authorization.
