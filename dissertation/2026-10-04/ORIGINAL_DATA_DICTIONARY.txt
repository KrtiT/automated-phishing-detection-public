AGGREGATE DATA DICTIONARY — October 1, 2026

GENERAL CONVENTIONS

CSV files are UTF-8 with a header. JSON is authoritative for nested structures,
explicit nulls/reasons and full-precision values. CSV blank fields mean absent,
inapplicable or undefined, not zero; consult the paired reason/status or JSON.
Boolean CSV values use True/False. List-valued CSV fields contain JSON arrays.

Rates and recall differences in data files are fractions, not percentages.
Multiply rates by 100 for percent and differences by 100 for percentage points.
Latency and drain times are milliseconds; throughput is requests per second.
Prevalence basis points: 10 = 0.1%, 100 = 1%, 500 = 5%.
Counts are URL/request/window counts, unless specifically named domains.
No prediction/URL-level research rows are supplied in these aggregate files.

PRIMARY RESULTS

primary-results.json: primary.metrics holds population-specific confusion
counts and rates; primary.contrasts holds all six clustered recall contrasts;
primary.hypotheses holds H1/H2/H3 completion, decision and component gates.
ablation_family is the fixed four-slot Holm family for nominal secondary
positive-only McNemar tests. It does not decide any primary hypothesis.

primary-gates.csv (22 rows): hypothesis and name identify the component.
estimate is the unrounded gate operand, including a lower interval endpoint
for contrast gates. operator and threshold specify the original decision rule.
numerator/denominator and upper_95 accompany rate gates when applicable.
upper_95 is an exact one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson bound, not the operand of
an observed-FPR gate. status is pass/fail; reason explains non-estimability
when applicable. H3 latency uses ms; other rate/difference gates use fractions.

paired-contrasts.csv (6 rows): estimate/lower/upper are URL-weighted paired
recall differences and domain-clustered 95% bootstrap interval endpoints.
candidate_true_positives/reference_true_positives/positive_count identify
the paired counts. domain_count is positive-stratum distinct domains.
bootstrap_replicates = 2,000, PCG64 seed 20260816, fresh RNG per contrast,
linear quantiles. Routing is not rerun in bootstrap replicates.

Nominal McNemar tables in complete-secondary-results.json retain aligned
positive-only paired decisions, discordances and exact two-sided p-values.
Holm uses all four fixed slots. Floating-point p = 0 denotes numeric
underflow, not mathematical impossibility. Domain/window/routing dependence
limits these nominal tests; they do not replace clustered primary intervals.

OPERATIONAL RESULTS

operational-runs.csv (125 rows): cell_ordinal/run_index/workload/concurrency/
prevalence_basis_points identify the frozen execution cell and manifest.
manifest_sha256 identifies its fixed request construction. Cells 1–72 are
retained; 73–125 are the continuation. No accepted adverse cell was replaced.
request_count/request_errors/request_error_rate count all measured client
terminal outcomes. p50_ms/p95_ms/p99_ms include failed measured requests.
admitted_requests/completed_requests/failed_requests are service observations.
transformer_forward_attempts and successful_transformer_scores are physical
service counters, not logical replay predictions.
client_attempts_per_second and successful_responses_per_second use each run's
measured client-phase elapsed interval. measured_elapsed_ms is that interval;
drain and timeout-drain durations are separate, not hidden in throughput.
primary_evidence preserves a source designation when present; blanks are not
permissions to substitute a different primary cell.

operational-groups.csv (25 rows): five repeats pooled by workload, manifest
and concurrency. run_indices/run_request_counts identify constituent runs.
Quantiles pool individual terminal latencies; they are not means of run
quantiles. physical_invocation_fraction = attempts / measured request_count.
Low transformer-only fractions can reflect failures/admission, not savings.
Cell 1 supplies the H3 invocation gate. Cells 21–25 supply H3 p95/error gates.
Live shift uses its actual 8,701 requests per repeat, not 10,000. Warmup,
model loading and control time are excluded. This is a closed-loop benchmark,
not a production capacity guarantee or an open-loop arrival experiment.

SECONDARY RESULTS

complete-secondary-results.json: original accepted internal/external secondary
structures, monitor windows, and three historical accepted aggregates with
their SHA-256s. Historical data carry their original audit limitations.

secondary-metrics.csv (153 rows): population/detector plus row/domain counts,
analysis_role, confusion counts, recall/FPR or control-alert counts, and
value/reason pairs for precision, F2, MCC, balanced accuracy, stepwise average
precision, ROC AUC, Brier score and ten-bin expected calibration error.
There are 43 mixed-class metric sets; single-class and label-free populations
do not receive invented two-class ranking/calibration metrics.

calibration-bins.csv (430 rows): ten bins per mixed-class metric set.
index identifies the fixed bin; count/positive_count/probability_sum/
squared_error_sum are sufficient aggregate totals. mean_probability and
positive_fraction describe nonempty bins. rows/domains describe the whole
population, not the bin.

low-fpr-score-curves.csv (43 rows): descriptive saved-score maximum recall
subject to observed FPR <= 1%, retaining score ties without interpolation.
threshold is the descriptive cutoff; true_positives/positive_count and
false_positives/negative_count give achieved rates. This is not a new
deployment threshold, retuning of a primary result or a passing primary gate.

prevalence-projections.csv (129 rows): assumed prevalence of 0.001, 0.01 or
0.05, reference_requests = 10,000, source_recall/source_fpr and the resulting
expected alerts/misses/false_alerts. These are arithmetic projections under
class-conditional rate transport, not actual traffic or measured HTTP counts.

source-contingency.csv (5 rows): role/source_group/source_class/confidence_tier
and reference-label counts, with row/domain totals. Tranco remains unlabeled.
This table does not identify unavailable PhiUSIIL per-record sources.

seed-logical-invocations.csv (35 rows): logical_band_count/rows and fraction
for each seed/population. These are saved-policy selections, not physical
HTTP measurements. Seed 42's historical runtime differs from later seeds.

external-monitor-windows.csv (396 rows): 132 windows per monitor, shared
original retained stream. start_position/end_position are inclusive 1-based
positions. score and threshold retain strict alert > threshold decisions;
reason records an undefined score when applicable. Windows contain 256 rows
at stride 64. End-of-stream partial windows do not enter the window fraction.

external-psi-features.csv (3,432 rows): 132 windows x 26 fixed features;
feature_index is zero-based. score is each feature's PSI component; threshold
is the saved aggregate monitor threshold, not a generic per-feature cutoff.

probe-decisions-and-scores.csv (20 rows): four development-audit streams x
five detector/policy columns. 00/01/10/11 are paired original-to-probe
decision transitions. positive_decisions is the probe-side alert count.
count, changed_score_count and signed/absolute deltas compare aligned scores.
probe-monitors-and-scores.csv (12 rows): four streams x three monitors, with
corresponding window-alert transitions, window counts, thresholds and deltas.
These probes are label-free; changes are not adversarial success or error.
