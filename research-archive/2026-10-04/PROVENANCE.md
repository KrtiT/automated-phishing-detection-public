## Chain of evidence

Publisher bytes → versioned preparation and domain-disjoint partitions →
development-only model/threshold selection → frozen evaluation → saved row-level
and request-level observations → verified reductions → manuscript tables and figures.

The [build plan](build-plan.json) enumerates the retained source roots. Each
archive inventory records original path, size and SHA-256, alongside public
size/hash and exact/projection/exclusion status. The release catalog binds those
inventories to archive checksums. Repository history identifies the source
algorithms and contracts; the package manifest identifies the displayed edition.
Hash identity is not evidence of institutional approval or an independently
replicated observation.

Original recovery accepts cells 1–72 from attempt 4 and complete cells 73–125
from the continuation. Earlier interrupted cells are preserved as historical
evidence, not counted as extra replications. The separate service recovery starts
a new full 80-arm schedule and does not pool its interrupted 45-arm predecessor.
Administrative metadata is published only as disclosed projections where needed;
private execution authority is neither granted nor revalidated by downloading it.
